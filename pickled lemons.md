Recipe:
Slice lemon and remove seeds. Mix salt and paprika. 
Put the lemon slice on the salt on both sides and in the cleaned jar, push them down.
they should take up 85% of the space.
Add lemon juice until it covers the lemons and use a lemon top to squish them down to the liquid. 
After a few days outside, burp the jar and replace the lemon top with olive oil.
Ready after a week or two.
