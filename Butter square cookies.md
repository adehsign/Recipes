Recipe:
230g unsalted butter, softened
1 egg
1/2 cup sugar
240g flour
1 tsp vanilla
1/2 tsp salt
sugar for dusting

preheat oven to 180. chill baking dish in freezer, do not grease the pan.

Mix butter, egg, sugar and salt until creamy. add flour and vanilla, and mix until holds together in large clumps.
flatten to a little less than 1 cm. dust with sugar. bake for around 12 minutes.
